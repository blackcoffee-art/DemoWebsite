🚗 Auto Insurance Plans

Auto Shield Basic

$50/month

What's Covered:

  • ✅ Collision Coverage — Repairs or replaces your vehicle after an accident, regardless of fault.
  • ✅ Liability Coverage — Up to $100,000 for bodily injury and property damage you cause to others.
  • ✅ Uninsured Motorist Protection — Covers you if the other driver has no insurance.
  • ✅ Medical Payments — Up to $5,000 per person for medical expenses after an accident.

What's NOT Covered:

  • ❌ Comprehensive events (hail, flood, theft, vandalism, animal strikes)
  • ❌ Roadside Assistance
  • ❌ Rental Car Reimbursement

Deductible: $1,000

Best For: Budget-conscious drivers who want essential protection for everyday driving.

MOST POPULAR

Auto Shield Plus

$85/month

What's Covered:

  • ✅ Everything in Auto Shield Basic
  • ✅ Comprehensive Coverage — Hail, flood, theft, vandalism, falling objects, animal strikes.
  • ✅ Roadside Assistance — Towing (up to 50 miles), flat tire change, battery jump, lockout service.
  • ✅ Rental Car Reimbursement — Up to $40/day for up to 14 days while your car is being repaired.
  • ✅ Gap Coverage — Pays the difference between your car's value and what you owe on your loan.
  • ✅ New Car Replacement — If your new car (less than 1 year old) is totaled, we replace it with a brand new one.

Deductible: $500

Best For: Drivers who want complete peace of mind and full protection.

🏠 Home Insurance Plans

Home Guardian

$120/month

What's Covered:

  • ✅ Fire & Smoke Damage — Full repair or rebuild cost.
  • ✅ Theft & Burglary — Covers stolen belongings up to $50,000.
  • ✅ Wind, Hail & Lightning Damage
  • ✅ Personal Liability — Up to $300,000 if someone is injured on your property.
  • ✅ Temporary Living Expenses — Hotel and meals if your home is uninhabitable (up to $15,000).
  • ✅ Personal Property — Covers furniture, electronics (up to $2,500 for electronics), clothing, and appliances.

What's NOT Covered:

  • ❌ Flood Damage (requires separate rider)
  • ❌ Earthquake Damage (requires separate rider)
  • ❌ Home Office Equipment above $2,500

Deductible: $1,500

Best For: Homeowners looking for solid, comprehensive property protection.

BEST VALUE

Home Guardian Premium

$180/month

What's Covered:

  • ✅ Everything in Home Guardian
  • ✅ Flood Coverage — Full protection against flood damage.
  • ✅ Earthquake Coverage — Structural and personal property coverage.
  • ✅ Home Office Equipment — Up to $10,000 for computers, monitors, desks, and office supplies.
  • ✅ Identity Theft Protection — Up to $25,000 in recovery costs and lost wages.
  • ✅ Increased Personal Property Limit — Up to $100,000.
  • ✅ Sewer & Water Backup Coverage

Deductible: $1,000

Best For: Homeowners in high-risk areas, those who work from home, or anyone who wants maximum protection.
